# Rubric

Scores are 0–10 with one decimal. Use the anchors; interpolate between them.
Overall = mean of the category scores (never a separate gut score).

| Category | 5 = | 7 = | 9 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typography | Sizes/weights mixed without a system; awkward line-heights | Coherent scale with minor inconsistencies | Tight type scale, clear roles, good rhythm |
| Hierarchy | Eye lands on chrome or metadata before content | Primary content mostly wins | Primary content and action unmistakable at a glance |
| Spacing & density | Cramped or sparse; uneven gaps | Consistent scale, a few off-grid spots | Every gap on-scale; density suits the task |
| Color & contrast | Noisy accents; low-contrast text | Restrained palette, a few weak contrasts | Color carries meaning only; WCAG AA or better everywhere |
| Iconography | Mixed sets or weights; decorative noise | One set, minor size/weight drift | Consistent, quiet, semantically clear |
| Consistency & polish | Visible misalignment; inconsistent radii/borders/states | Mostly consistent | Pixel-tidy; states cohesive across screens |
| Clarity | Unclear what state you're in or what to do next | Mostly clear | State and next action legible immediately |

## Adapting

Add or swap categories for the product (e.g. "Data legibility" for dashboards,
"Motion" if you capture short clips). Keep 5–8 categories and write all three
anchors for any new one before round 1. Never change the rubric mid-loop — that
invalidates the comparison with earlier rounds.
